Status Current state:Under Discussion Discussion thread:https://lists.apache.org/thread/njjznyly38td4scb9bjc8v0gq7kc1yqj JIRA:here[Change the link from KAFKA-1 to your own ticket] Motivation The long-standing problem: silent data loss on partition expansion Increasing a topic's partition count is a routine operational task, not an exceptional event. Topics are often created with a moderate number of partitions to balance cost and resource utilization, then expanded as workloads grow. When a topic is expanded, a consumer configured withauto.offset.reset=latestcan silently miss every record produced to the newly added partitions before the consumer becomes aware of them. The sequence is straightforward: The topic is expanded, and the new partitions immediately begin receiving records. The consumer does not learn about the new partitions until its next metadata refresh or assignment update. This creates a short metadata-blindness window. When the consumer is assigned the new partitions, no committed offsets exist for them. As a result,auto.offset.resetapplies.
