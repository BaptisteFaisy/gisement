Motivation Apache Kafka MirrorMaker 2 provides cross-cluster replication for Kafka topics and consumer group offsets. Currently, offset synchronisation is handled by the MirrorCheckpointConnector, which periodically translates and emits consumer group offsets from source to the target cluster. Also, it optionally automatically commits consumer group offsets to the target cluster. The offset translation mechanism relies on two parallel processes: MirrorSourceConnector tasks periodically produce offset-sync records, which MirrorCheckpointConnector tasks then consume to build and maintain an in-memory cache of partition offset mappings.
