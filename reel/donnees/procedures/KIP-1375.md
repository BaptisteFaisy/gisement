Status Current state:"Under Discussion" Discussion thread:here(not made yet) JIRA:here Motivation The interfaceStateRestoreListenerhas a methodonRestoreSuspendedwhich lets a client observe the restoration of aStateStorewhen thatStateStore's restoration is suspended before it is finished. StateRestoreListener.onRestoreSuspendedcurrently does not provide the reason for the restoration being suspended. The suspension can happen for one of three reasons: the task was assigned to another instance the task was demoted to a standby task on this instance the instance is shutting down AStateRestoreListeneris currently unable to differentiate between these reasons even though these reasons may be useful for a Kafka user who, for example, wants to emit observability to diagnose their application.
