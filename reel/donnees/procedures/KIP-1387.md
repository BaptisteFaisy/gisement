Motivation Kafka clients report latency and size as an average and a maximum over a 30-second window,and nothing elsereaches aMetricsReporter.clients/src/main/javausesnew Avg()36 times,new Max()31 times,andPercentilesnever,so no client latency or size metric has a percentile today. An average and a maximum are useful,but they don't show how the values are spread out.Say a producer's requestsmostly take 3 to 7 ms,and one request in the window stalls for 2 seconds.The average jumps to about 150 ms,whichno longer looks like a normal request.Neither number tells you that most requests finished under 7 ms,or whetherthe stall was a one-off or the start of a problem.Fetch sizes have the same problem:they stay small while aconsumer keeps up and jump when it reads a backlog,so the average lands between the two but does not represent either scenario (low steady state and peaks during lag). You can't work out p50 or p99 from an average and a maximum.Kafk…
