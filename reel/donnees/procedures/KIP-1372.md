Motivation Kafka currently accepts anymin.insync.replicasvalue of 1 or more, with no relation to the replication factor of the topics it applies to. A topic can be created with--replication-factor 1 --config min.insync.replicas=2and the request succeeds, even though the configuration is contradictory: the topic can never have enough in-sync replicas to satisfy the configured minimum. This misconfiguration is not hypothetical. It has been reported since 2017 (KAFKA-4680), where producing withacks=allto such a topic fails with theretriableNotEnoughReplicasException(retries that can never succeed), and the resulting error-log flood filled a broker's disk in a production cluster. Replication tooling that creates topics on a destination cluster whose defaultmin.insync.replicasexceeds the source topic's replication factor hits the same trap, and the failure surfaces far from its cause.
