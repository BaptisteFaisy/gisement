Motivation Rack-aware assignment is costly and, in the common case, changes nothing. Yet the group coordinator pays for it for every group. Rack-aware assignment gives each member partitions with a replica in its rack, so that the member can fetch from that replica instead of crossing racks (KIP-392). It changes nothing in two common cases: Fetch from follower is not used, so the members fetch from the leader. It is off by default for consumers, and share consumers do not support it. The replicas of every partition already cover all racks, as with three availability zones and a replication factor of three. Neither the coordinator nor the assignor can tell which case applies without doing the work that would be wasted. The assignor.Rack awareness is planned for the built-in consumer assignors (KAFKA-19387).
