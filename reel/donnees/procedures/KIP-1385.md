Status Current state:"Under Discussion" Discussion thread:here JIRA:here Related:KAFKA-20609/PR #22873(the current last-modified fallback; design discussion in that PR's review thread), [KAFKA-21158](documents and warns about raising `message.timestamp.after.max.ms`) Motivation In a tiered topic, a local segment is deleted by time, once it has been uploaded to remote storage and condition 'now - largestRecordTimestamp > local.retention.ms' succeeds. The age is measured from record timestamps, which producers set. This causes two problems: 1. Old timestamps For records produced with timestamps older than local.retention.ms, the local copy is deleted as soon as the segment is uploaded to remote storage. The configured local window is zero for this data, for example during backfills or replays. Example: local.retention.ms is 1 hour. At 10:00, a backfill writes records stamped 07:00 the same day. The segment is uploaded at 10:05. At the next retention check, the records are already about 3 hours old, so the local copy is deleted right away.
