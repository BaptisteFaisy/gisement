Motivation Brokers already expose per-client quota usagebyte-rate,request-timeand throttle timethrottle-time. They do not expose how that usage compares to the effective quota applied to the same quota sensor. That join is left to operators: scrape the rate, then independently resolve user, client-id, and default overrides (or a customClientQuotaCallback)from a different path. Limits can differ by orders of magnitude and can change dynamically, so absolute-rate alerts do not transfer across clients. throttle-timeonly changes after throttling has occurred. Operators need a normalized rate signal that identifies clients whose recent usage is approaching or exceeding the quota actually applied on that broker, without requiring the monitoring stack to resolve the quota independently.
