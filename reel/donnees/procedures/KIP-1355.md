Motivation KIPs likeKIP-392: Allow consumers to fetch from closest replica,KIP-1150: Diskless Topics, andKIP-1123: Rack-aware partitioning for Kafka Producer, apart from other goals, aim to reduce inter-rack traffic. This brings potential benefits such as lower latency and costs, especially in cloud environments where inter-rack (i.e. inter-availability zone) traffic is often billed. While these initiatives succeed in this goal, the aspect of observability could be improved. Knowing how much traffic goes within a rack and how much crosses rack borders would be valuable to operators, for example, to highlight misconfiguration. This KIP proposes to close this gap with additional metrics.
