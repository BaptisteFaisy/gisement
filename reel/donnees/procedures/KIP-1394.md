Motivation Under the Streams rebalance protocol, members send topology metadata when joining a group. The group coordinator compares this metadata with the topology already registered for the group to verify that the member uses the same topology. Each time a member joins or rejoins, the coordinator repeats structural comparisons of topology metadata that is typically identical across members. For Streams groups with large and complex topologies, these comparisons can impose a substantial workload on the coordinator.
