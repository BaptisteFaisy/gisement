Motivation max.poll.recordsandmax.poll.interval.msare read when a consumer is constructed and neither can be changedafterwards. If processing slows down,a batch that used to fit inside the poll interval stops fitting.The consumer misses itsdeadline and leaves the group.The assignment is released throughonPartitionsLost,so the in-flight batch was nevercommitted and the next owner reprocesses it. The consumer rejoins and asks for the same batch size without any changes to config.It misses the deadline again.This continues until the slowdown ends.A shared dependency degrades for all members at once,so they hit it togetherand the group rebalances repeatedly.The common response is a process restart after increasingmax.poll.interval.msor decreasingmax.poll.records,which reassigns every group in that JVM.
