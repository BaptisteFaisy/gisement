Motivation introduced share groups and included thekafka-share-groups.shtool for initializing and resetting the share-partition start offsets. This was inspired by the equivalentkafka-consumer-groups.shtool for consumer groups, but it did not include all of the options for resetting options which are available for consumer groups. When users are migrating from a consumer group to a share group, it is natural that they would want to initialize the share group with the committed offsets from the consumer group. Programmatically, this is easy, but there is no way usingkafka-share-groups.shto achieve this. This KIP adds extra options to that tool to make this kind of migration easy. Proposed Changes Additional options are added tokafka-share-groups.shto give more flexibility for initialization of share group offsets.
