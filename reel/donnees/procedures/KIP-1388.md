Motivation Tools that replicate consumer group offsets between clusters typically use two RPCs, with client-side filtering in between: Admin.listGroups(ListGroupsOptions.forConsumerGroups())(theListGroupsRPC, sent to every broker) to enumerate the consumer groups on the source cluster. Apply a user-supplied group filter (an allow list, deny list or pattern) to the listed group ids. Admin.listConsumerGroupOffsets(...)(theOffsetFetchRPC, batched per group coordinator) for the groups that passed the filter. MirrorMaker 2 does exactly this inMirrorCheckpointConnector, and the group offset synchronization in KIP-1279 (Cluster Mirroring) follows the same pattern. Other cross-cluster replication tools built on the Admin API do the same. A group exists in the group coordinator while it has at least one member or at least one committed offset; a group with neither is removed by the periodic group cleanup.
