Motivation Share groups redeliver a released record immediately, so a transient failure can use up all of a record's delivery attempts immediately. This KIP lets a share group wait before redelivering such a record, with an optional exponential backoff that grows with each attempt. With share groups (KIP-932), a consumer that cannot process a record acknowledges it withAcknowledgeType.RELEASE. The record is then available for another delivery attempt straight away. The same happens when the record's acquisition lock times out, or when the consumer holding it disconnects. Immediate redelivery suits failures that are unlikely to recur, such as a consumer crash.
