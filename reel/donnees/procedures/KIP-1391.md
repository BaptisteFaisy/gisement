Applications may subsequently need to process a selected subset of those records again without repeating unrelated completed work. Examples include correcting a transformation, reprocessing inputs affected by an application defect, or restoring the exact inputs recorded in a durable processing manifest. Existing mechanisms do not directly support this requirement: RELEASE relinquishes currently acquired records; it does not reopen previously completed records.
