Status Current state: Under Discussion Discussion thread: https://lists.apache.org/thread/gctkxrg2y664jt4sq18znrgl0omd93tp JIRA: KAFKA-20848 Release: TBD Motivation With the consumer group protocol (KIP-848), share groups (KIP-932) and the Streams rebalance protocol (KIP-1071), settings that shape how a client behaves are group configurations owned by the broker. Some of them, such as the session timeout and the heartbeat interval of consumer groups, used to be client configurations; others, such as a share group's record lock duration, were introduced as group configurations. Settings such as session timeouts and heartbeat intervals have broker-level defaults and bounds, and an administrator can override them for a single group. None of these values are set in the client's configuration. The client sees only some of these values. The heartbeat responses of all three protocols carry the heartbeat interval, and the Streams heartbeat response also carries the task offset interval and the acceptable recovery lag.
